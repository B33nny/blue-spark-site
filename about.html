About us

Technology should adapt to people, not the other way around.

Real automation has been locked behind big enterprise budgets. We don't think the future is about replacing people with machines. It's about scaling what your people already have: deep knowledge of their own industry. With Blue Spark, you teach the AI your expertise and automate your work from the inside out.

What we believe

Three ideas behind everything we build.

Private by default

Your work belongs to you. Blue Spark lives on your own laptop, and your documents stay there.

Checked, not claimed

People trust technology they can understand. So Blue Spark checks its work and shows you what it did.

Built with people

We teach, we listen, and we build alongside the professionals who use Blue Spark every day.

The founders

The people behind Blue Spark.

Benjamin Yeo

Founder and Chief Architect, Blue Spark Ventures

Ben brings more than 20 years of commercial leadership, strategic partnerships and business development across Asia-Pacific. He has built multimillion-dollar partnerships with C-suite executives and government leaders across ASEAN, and led teams of more than 250 professionals.

At Blue Spark he combines that commercial experience with hands-on work in generative AI, automation and marketing technology. His focus is AI that removes wasted effort and lets businesses grow without growing headcount at the same rate.

"Technology should no longer require people to adapt to it. It should understand and adapt to people."

Ben's guiding philosophy

Dr. Mogana Sundaram N. Narayanam

Co-Founder and Strategic Director, Blue Spark Ventures

Dr. Mogana brings more than 40 years of international corporate leadership, enterprise technology rollouts and workforce development. His career includes senior roles with ExxonMobil, ESSO Angola and PETRONAS, across Malaysia, Angola and the United States.

He co-authored the ExxonMobil Competency Assurance Standards Version 1.0, trained more than 1,500 industry professionals, and led digital rollouts such as eWork Permitting and SAP for offshore operations. At Blue Spark he directs commercial strategy, partnerships and market development.

"Technology creates lasting value only when people understand it, trust it, and are empowered to use it effectively."

Dr. Mogana's guiding philosophy
