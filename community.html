A community that puts ideas to work.

Blue Spark’s founding community brings people from different professions together around a shared aim: learning to turn real work into useful automation.

Bring something from your working day.

A repeated task, a stubborn process, or an idea for a better way to serve customers. The most useful starting point is the work you understand.

Learn through conversation and practice.

Founder-led group Zoom sessions provide a place to ask questions, understand the approach, and apply it to your own industry. Our partner coordinates the sessions and the practical arrangements.

Contribute to what comes next.

Members will help inform the skills marketplace planned for 2027. We want to hear what makes a skill useful, what creators need, and what buyers need to trust it.

Respect the room.

Be constructive. Protect client and member confidentiality. Share only information you are entitled to share. Give others room to learn. The community is a place to develop capability, not a promise of clients, income, or permanent founder availability.
