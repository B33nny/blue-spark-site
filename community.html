Good morning

Running AI on everyday text and images got a lot cheaper this week. Sorting emails, reading forms and checking photos are now realistic jobs to automate, even for a small business.

Five things to know

01
Try

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, a faster and cheaper small model.

Why it matters
High-volume jobs like sorting documents and tagging emails cost much less to run.
Where the money is
Service providers can build low-cost sorting tools for local businesses.

Try

Why it matters
It can run on a strong laptop, so photos never leave your premises.
Where the money is

Automate this

Sort your support inbox automatically.

The problem
Someone has to read every support email, decide what it is and pass it on.
The build
Connect the inbox to a small, cheap AI model that tags each email by department and forwards it.
Keep a human for
Complex complaints, upset customers and refunds.

Business opportunity

A weekly shelf-planning service for regional shops.

Store managers rarely have time to compare sales with what's happening around them. Offer a weekly, local stock and display recommendation, powered by small AI models.

Test it cheaply: run one store's past sales through an open model as a free pilot.

Today's practical action

Pick one weekly task that involves reading and sorting, like supplier emails, and work out what it costs you per month.
