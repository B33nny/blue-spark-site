Everyday Inference

Anthropic Drops Pricing 75% with Claude Haiku 5.5

By Technology and Industry Correspondent CONFIRMED

What happened: Anthropic launched Claude Haiku 5.5, a fast, small-tier model featuring a 1-million-token context window and built-in adaptive thinking.

Why it matters: It dramatically lowers costs for high-volume tasks like document processing, classification, and sub-agent routing. Estimates indicate the model is roughly 75% cheaper to run than its predecessor, Haiku 4.5, making automated sorting of massive datasets viable for smaller budgets.

Details: Global availability via Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Foundry. Priced at $0.10 per 1 million input tokens and $0.50 per 1 million output tokens (under 100,000 tokens).

Verdict: TRY — service providers can build highly affordable, high-volume classification tools for local businesses. (Confidence: CONFIRMED)

2

Local Vision

Reka Edge 2603 Makes Vision AI Hyper-Efficient

By Technology and Industry Correspondent CONFIRMED

What happened: Reka AI shipped Edge 2603, a 7-billion parameter vision-language model optimized for devices with limited memory.

Why it matters: The model encodes a 1024x1024 image using only 331 tokens — significantly fewer than competitors. This makes visual AI much faster and cheaper to run locally on standard hardware (e.g. a 24GB unified-memory machine).

Details: Free for local deployment; API pricing starts at $0.05 to $0.10 per 1 million tokens. Target users include hardware deployers and teams with strict data-privacy needs.

Verdict: TRY — deploy locally for offline retail inventory tracking, ensuring sensitive floor data never leaves premises. (Confidence: CONFIRMED)

OpenAI Introduces Generative Interfaces with GPT-6

Fujitsu Deploys Retail-Specific AI Agents

Capability Shift: Visual QA Cost and Efficiency

Before · 6 months ago

Processing 10,000 images for visual quality assurance required expensive frontier models, costing hundreds of dollars and taking substantial processing time.

Now · October 2026

Advanced vision has become hyper-efficient: a new baseline encodes a 1024x1024 image in 331 tokens versus about 1,094 before, with starting costs near $0.05 per 1 million tokens, so operations can affordably digitize massive archives daily.

What to do: Move image-heavy quality assurance onto efficient local vision models and reserve frontier models for the hard cases.
