National semiconductor sovereignty · CONFIRMED

Malaysia unveils first six homegrown semiconductor chips from Puchong IC Design Park

By Technology & Industry Correspondent — Quartz Technology Dispatch

Puchong, Selangor — Malaysia's Ministry of Economy and domestic semiconductor leaders officially unveiled the country's first six homegrown integrated circuit (IC) chips, designed by local engineering teams at the Malaysia Semiconductor IC Design Park in Puchong.

Why it matters: For decades, Malaysia's tech sector was confined to outsourced assembly and testing (OSAT). Designing proprietary silicon locally provides the foundation for domestic edge-AI hardware, IoT controllers, and embedded computing — reducing reliance on foreign chip designers.

Community Opportunity: Position regional software agencies to develop edge-AI firmware and middleware optimized specifically for homegrown Malaysian microcontrollers and IoT silicon.

Self-hosted office AI · CONFIRMED

ONLYOFFICE 10.0 integrates self-hosted, context-aware AI across documents and sheets

By Enterprise Systems Desk — Markets Business Insider

Open-source office suite ONLYOFFICE announced and rolled out Version 10.0, embedding context-aware AI capabilities natively into its word processor, spreadsheet, and presentation applications, with support for private on-premise LLMs (such as Ollama or local API endpoints).

Many professional practices in Malaysia and Singapore cannot adopt Microsoft 365 Copilot due to cross-border data transfer restrictions under the PDPA or high subscription costs. ONLYOFFICE 10.0 provides an on-premise collaborative suite where document data never leaves the local network.

Who can use: Law firms, healthcare clinics, accounting practices, and privacy-conscious SMEs.
