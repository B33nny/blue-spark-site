Before you install.

Confirm that your computer meets the requirements listed for this release. You will need the account or licence details associated with your purchase. Some capabilities require an internet connection or access to configured services.

Install and activate.

  1. Download from the official release link.

  2. Open the installer and follow the displayed instructions.

  3. Complete the activation steps for your paid access, then open Blue Spark and begin with a task you understand.

Check the release.

Review the version, publication date, file details, and verification information shown below. The release notes explain what has changed.
