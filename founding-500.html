Who it's for

You already have a valuable starting point.

You understand your industry. The tasks people repeat, the exceptions that matter, and the difference between a rough draft and work somebody would actually use.

You don't need to be a developer. Bring that understanding, and one thing you'd like to improve.

How it works

Three steps, at your own pace.

  1. Step 1

    Pick a task you know well

    A preparation routine, a research process, a document you redo every week. Start with something that costs you time.

  2. Step 2

    Build it with Ben and the group

    Join live group classes on Zoom or Google Meet. Bring your workflow, ask questions and learn from how other professionals solve similar problems.

  3. Step 3

    Help shape the marketplace

    As your skill takes shape, you may see something other people could use. Founding members help decide what the skills marketplace should be.
