Make your expertise go further.

The Blue Spark Founding 500.

Make your own work easier. Learn to build useful automation. Discover where your knowledge could take you next.

Apply for Founding 500

You already have a valuable starting point.

You understand your industry. The tasks people repeat. The exceptions that matter. The difference between a rough output and work somebody would actually use.

You do not have to arrive as a developer. Bring that understanding — and something you want to improve.

Learn with the person building Blue Spark.

Ben will lead interactive group Zoom sessions, coordinated by our partner, to help members approach automation in their own industries.

Bring a recurring workflow. Ask questions. Work through a useful example. Learn from the way other professionals solve related problems.

The aim is practical capability you can apply, not another collection of videos you never find time to finish.

Start with your own time. Then consider the opportunity.

A preparation routine. A research process. A recurring document. Build around a task you know well.

As you develop that capability, you may see a service or skill other people could use. Your professional experience can become the foundation for more than your own daily output.

Useful expertise is the opportunity. Clients and earnings are not guaranteed.

A voice in the marketplace ahead.

A Blue Spark skills marketplace is planned for 2027. Founding members will help shape it through ideas and feedback about what creators and customers actually need.

The marketplace is not live today. This is an invitation to contribute to its development, not a promise of immediate listings or sales.

The founding offer.

$120 per month. Professional access, with the founding programme's teaching, participation, and two-year licence-price protection.

Currency, taxes and included usage are confirmed at the offer before payment.

Limited to 500 founding members. A community built around practical learning, different industries, and useful work.

Founder-led group learning. Sessions are coordinated by our partner. Membership is not unlimited private mentoring or permanent on-demand access to Ben.
