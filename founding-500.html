The founding terms.

Membership is $120 per month. Currency, taxes and the included usage allowance are confirmed at checkout. The published price terms are not finalised yet.

The founding programme includes two-year protection of the monthly licence price under the published founding terms. Founder-led interaction takes place through structured group sessions; it is not unlimited on-demand support or a guaranteed private consultancy.

A place for people who put ideas to work.

You do not have to arrive as a technical expert. Come with an understanding of your work, a willingness to learn, and respect for other members’ time and information.

What membership does not promise.

Membership does not guarantee income, customers, marketplace listings, equity, or lifetime founder access. The value is the opportunity to learn, build, contribute, and develop useful capability.
