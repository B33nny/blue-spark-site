More time for the life you're building.

A business you are proud of. Clients who get your full attention. An idea that finally gets its chance. An evening that belongs to you.

You do not have to want the same things as everyone else. You just need a little more room for the things that matter to you.

Blue Spark helps with the work that keeps filling that room — the research, the documents, the preparation, and the repeated tasks around your day.

Dependable help for your work. More room for your life.

The ambition is yours. Not every task has to be.

Being good at what you do can leave you with more people, more decisions, and more details depending on you.

You become the adviser and the administrator. The creator and the organiser. The person with the idea — and the person still formatting the document at the end of the day.

Blue Spark is a personal AI assistant built to help carry that surrounding work. Not another place to leave a question. A way to move from a conversation towards a useful result.

Meet your assistant

An assistant of your own.

Explain what you need, as you would to someone helping you. Bring the relevant information. Describe what good work looks like.

Blue Spark brings conversation, remembered context, and practical computer tools together to help carry it through.

Your standards remain yours. Your assistant helps you put them into practice.

What would you hand over first?

The preparation before the important conversation. Organise project notes into a client brief, with the questions and decisions you want in front of you.

The work around your next idea. Gather research, organise a transcript, or shape rough material into a first draft.

The routine that takes another piece of every week. Start with a recurring report, document, or information-handling task you know well.

You do not need to change your whole business to begin. Start with one part of the day you would rather not carry alone.

Trust belongs in the working relationship.

An assistant should be clear about what it remembers, what it is doing, and what still needs your attention.

Blue Spark is built around that standard: useful memory, understandable control, and an honest distinction between progress and completion.

Our local-first approach keeps persistent memory on your computer. Selected task information can be sent to cloud services when they are used. We explain that distinction because your trust deserves more than a slogan.
