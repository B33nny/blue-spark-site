01 — The facts

Five things it does. No marketing language.

Runs on your machine.

No GPU upgrade. The office laptop you already own is the office laptop you already need.

Remembers you in five layers.

Working. Episodic. Semantic. Procedural. Persona.

Low latency, scaled to your hardware.

We don't publish a millisecond benchmark. Speed is real, and it is a function of your disk and CPU.

No cloud round-trip.

Voice, conversations, and memory never leave the disk.

Free base product.

Pro is $120/month. The base experience is uncrippled and uncapped.

02 — The work

Who it's for.

Three audience cards. Each one names the workflow, not the buyer.

Legal

IP lawyers and niche legal practitioners

Case files. Prior art. Claim charts. Evidence binders. Docketing calendars.

The work is dense, the confidentiality floor is high, and the retention curve once installed is the longest in our audience.

Finance

Banking, analysts, accountants

Balance sheets. Financial models. Audit prep. Multi-source reconciliation.

Heavy spreadsheet work. The clearest office-automation win in the product. Persistent memory across fiscal periods is the difference between remembering and starting over.

Content

Copywriters and content creators

Drafts. Revisions. References. Brand voice notes.

The work is inherently busy, unstructured, and messy. Voice and personality matter most here. Persona-layer engagement. High social-share potential.

If your day doesn't read like one of these, Blue Spark probably isn't for you.

03 — The line

Who it's NOT for.

This is the section most sites skip. We lead with it.

  • Cloud-first users who want a chat app on every device.
  • The general medical field. Deliberately bypassed during the initial rollout — historically resistant to non-prescribed tech adoption, and the consent and liability surface is too wide for a v1 product to credibly cover.
  • Anyone shopping for the latest foundation-model benchmarks. We are not in that race.
  • Anyone who wants a free chatbot to "try out AI." Blue Spark is a working tool, not a demo.
  • Subscription-comfortable users who reflexively want a monthly consumer subscription. The base product is free; Pro is positioned as the cost of keeping your spark bright, not as a SaaS bill.
