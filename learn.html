You know the work. Start with that.

You do not need to learn everything about AI before you begin. Start with one task, describe the result you need, and build from a useful first outcome.

  • Describe the job clearly.

    Explain the starting information, the action you want, and what a good result should contain. “Help with my business” is broad. “Use these notes to prepare a client-meeting brief with open questions” gives the work a shape.
