Get Blue Spark

Your expertise could travel further.

The Blue Spark skills marketplace. Planned for 2027.

A future place for professional knowledge to become useful automation for other people.

You know something worth putting to work.

The sequence that prevents a mistake. The information a good report needs. The way to prepare a client pack without forgetting the important details.

Our creator direction is to help professionals turn that knowledge into Blue Spark skills and workflows others can use.

The platform remains Blue Spark. You bring the industry expertise and the service around it.

Your customers. Your hard work.

Our commercial principle is simple: Blue Spark takes no cut when you sell your own Blue Spark skills or automations directly to your own customers.

You do not have to wait for a marketplace to build your own customer relationships. The Blue Spark software licence and the creator's skill or service remain separate arrangements.

When the marketplace opens a new door.

A buyer elsewhere may discover your skill through Blue Spark. Where the marketplace helps bring the customer and enables a sale or rental, a platform share forms part of the intended model.

The detailed fees, listing arrangements, and transaction terms are still being developed. No sales, income, or marketplace placement are guaranteed.
