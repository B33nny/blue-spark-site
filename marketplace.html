The Blue Spark Marketplace

A marketplace where the people who built the skill own it.

You have spent a decade becoming the person in your firm who knows what the binder means, what the reconciliation needs, or what the brief missed.

That knowledge is your intellectual property. The Marketplace is where you list that knowledge as a compiled skill the assistant can use.

Marketplace in development

The Marketplace is being built. These are the rights and economics we plan to offer.

Your rights. Your control.

  • You wrote it, you own it.

    Skill code, skill name, pricing, and license terms belong to you. When a buyer installs your skill, it runs exclusively on their local machine, against their private data.

  • You can take it back.

    Every skill carries a modern digital signature verifying provenance. License checks are minimal network lookups. If a creator chooses to revoke access to a skill for any reason, the subsequent license check disables it globally. Your intellectual property remains under absolute control.

Governance & revenue.

  • Community-Voted Economics

    The monetization model is decided by the people who use it. Early adopters will utilize a quadratic voting mechanism to determine platform fees, revenue splits, quality bars, and payout cadences.

  • Voice Credits

    Each Pro subscriber receives 100 voice-credits to allocate across topics. Creators with submitted skills receive an additional 50 credits.
