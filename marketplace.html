Your expertise can become something others put to work.

Planned for 2027

We are developing a marketplace where people can discover Blue Spark skills created around real professional knowledge. The Founding 500 will help shape how it is built.

Expertise is the starting point.

You understand the details of your industry: the sequence, the exceptions, the information that matters, and what good work looks like. Our creator direction is to help turn that knowledge into useful automation for other people.

Your customers. Your direct skill sales.

When you sell your own skills or automation directly to your own customers, Blue Spark takes no cut of that direct skill sale. The customer’s underlying Blue Spark licence remains a separate requirement under its own terms.

Still Blue Spark—prepared for their work.

Our intended delivery model allows creators to prepare Blue Spark with their own skills for customers. The platform keeps the Blue Spark name; the creator adds the expertise, configuration, and services that make it relevant to the customer’s work.

When the marketplace brings the customer.

A marketplace can help a buyer discover expertise they would not otherwise find. Where Blue Spark creates that connection and enables the transaction, a platform share may apply. The detailed commercial terms are still being developed.

Licensing with Jade Seal.

Jade Seal is intended to support the licensing of creator skills. The sale, rental, maintenance, and customer-access arrangements will be explained when the corresponding marketplace features and terms are ready.

Help us build something worth using.

Bring your ideas through the founding community. We want a marketplace informed by the people who will create the skills, maintain them, and use them in real work.
