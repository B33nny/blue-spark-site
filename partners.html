Bring useful automation to the people you already serve.

We welcome conversations with businesses that can introduce, deploy, or distribute Blue Spark to relevant professional customers.

A distribution relationship—not a referral link.

A partnership should explain who the customer is, how they will be supported, and the role each business will play. The commercial terms depend on the arrangement.

Expertise makes the relationship useful.

Your knowledge of an industry or customer group can help make automation practical. Where you develop your own skills or services, the Blue Spark platform and your contribution should remain clearly distinguished.

Buying for your own team?

An internal organizational deployment is different from distributing Blue Spark to customers. Our enterprise route is the right place to discuss your own workforce and systems.
