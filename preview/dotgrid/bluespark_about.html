Why BlueSpark™ exists

Automation, without the fortune.

Automating your work usually costs a fortune. BlueSpark™ changes that. If you have a computer, you can automate your daily work easily, without changing the systems you already use.

What we believe

Three things we hold to.

01

Your data stays yours.

The big AI companies harvest your data and use it to build their own empires. A 2025 Stanford study found that six leading U.S. AI developers feed what people type back into their own models, and some offer no way to opt out. BlueSpark™ is different. Your data stays on your machine, and everything it learns belongs to you.

02

Your experience can’t be replaced.

We believe your experience as a professional can never be replaced by AI. That’s why we’re inviting 500 professionals to become the Founding 500, sharing their experience with BlueSpark™ to help automate their own industries.

03

AI should prove itself.

AI forgets, hallucinates, and says the job is done when it isn’t. When AI hallucinates, it’s the people who pay the price. So with BlueSpark™, the AI suggests and BlueSpark™ decides.

Technology should adapt to people, not the other way around.

The founders

The people behind BlueSpark™.

Benjamin Yeo

Founder and Chief Architect, Blue Spark Ventures

Ben brings more than 20 years of commercial leadership, strategic partnerships and business development across Asia-Pacific. He has built multimillion-dollar partnerships with C-suite executives and government leaders across ASEAN, and led teams of more than 250 professionals.

“Technology should no longer require people to adapt to it. It should understand and adapt to people.”

Ben’s guiding philosophy

Dr. Mogana Sundaram N. Narayanam

Co-Founder and Strategic Director, Blue Spark Ventures
