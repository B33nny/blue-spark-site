Why $120/month — the anchoring math

Four anchors. Pick the one that matches your day.

Against ChatGPT Plus — $20/month

Six times the price, for a different category of tool. Plus is a chat window in a browser tab. Blue Spark is a persistent colleague that lives on your machine, remembers you in five layers, and works the files on your disk.

Against ChatGPT Team — $25–60/user/month

Comparable price for a tool that owns the whole workflow instead of one window of it. If your firm already pays per-seat for AI, Pro lands inside the range you already accept.

Against a junior contractor — $2,000–4,000/month

Six percent of the cost, for the document work a junior spends most of their day on: pulling contradictions, reconciling lines, tightening drafts. The judgment stays with you. The grunt work doesn't.

Against your recovered time

An hour a day returned to real work is the anchor that matters for most people. At any professional billing rate, an hour a day pays for Pro before the first week closes.

Why we charge anything

We are not subsidizing ourselves with user data. We are not running ads. We are not training models on conversations. The product has a real cost. The user pays that cost directly.

Why the free tier exists

The free tier is uncrippled by design. No nag screens. The upgrade happens because the user wants it, not because we forced it. A tool you can use free forever has to earn its price every month — that pressure makes the product better.
