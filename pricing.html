A few things to know.

Is this a monthly payment?

Yes. Blue Spark is paid recurring monthly access. Your offer identifies the currency, taxes, included usage, and any separately priced services before payment.

Can a person or company buy licences?

Yes. Licences can be held by a person or company. Additional licences can be purchased and assigned under the applicable licence terms.

Is usage unlimited?

The offer sets out the usage included in your plan. The monthly amount is not a promise of unlimited use of every model or connected service.

Is there a free trial?

No. Blue Spark is a paid professional product. Explore how it helps and ask about the fit for your work before purchasing.

What is the refund policy?

Our standard commercial policy is no discretionary refunds, subject to rights that cannot be excluded by applicable law. Report a service issue so we can investigate and address it.

What happens when paid access ends?

Access continues through the end of the paid period, followed by an automatic seven-day grace period. Your access terms set out the features and usage available during and after that period.
