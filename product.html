Begin in your own words.

Describe the job as you would to someone helping you. Give the context, explain the desired result, and correct anything that needs adjusting. Conversation is the starting point—not a technical qualification.

Put your existing work to use.

Bring the documents, information, and supported applications involved in the task. Blue Spark is designed to work where your work already happens, rather than require you to recreate the business inside a new platform.

Remember what matters.

Persistent memory can bring useful details back across sessions. Your preferences, recurring information, and previous work can help supply context for the next job.

Less supervising. More progress.

Our approach to automation is straightforward: once you have granted standing permission for cloud assistance, ordinary authorized model calls should not stop the job to ask the same permission again.

Initiative with a purpose.

We are building Blue Spark to recognize useful next steps from the work and context you have entrusted to it. Initiative should reduce the burden on the owner, not create more questions or take control away from them.

In production Planned for 2027

An assistant shaped around your work.

The longer-term direction is a worker with increasing familiarity, reusable skills, and a distinct working style shaped by its owner. We distinguish that direction from the capabilities available in each release.
