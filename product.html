Meet your new assistant.

Help that becomes familiar with your work.

Blue Spark is your personal and professional AI assistant — built to help with the work, not just talk about it.

See how it helps See pricing

The kind of support you wish you could hire.

An assistant who knows the background. Helps with the preparation. Remembers how you prefer things done.

That is the working relationship behind Blue Spark: a personal AI with the practical role of an AI employee, helping you carry more of the day without becoming another person to chase.

Start with a conversation.

"Use these notes to prepare me for tomorrow's client meeting."

"Turn this transcript into a first draft, keeping my main argument."

"Help me organise the information for this week's report."

You bring the task and your expectations. Refine the result in ordinary language. You do not need to begin with a workflow diagram or a programming lesson.

Help with the doing, too.

Research gathered. Notes organised. Documents prepared. Work carried through supported browser and desktop tools.

Blue Spark connects the instruction with the actions needed to produce a useful result. The conversation is the beginning; the work is the reason for it.

Familiarity makes the next task easier to explain.

Relevant details from previous conversations can return when you need them. Your preferred style, project background, and earlier decisions can contribute to the next piece of work.

The value is not simply a longer history. It is having useful context at hand.

Your industry. Your way of working.

Start with a process you understand: what comes in, what should happen, and what a good result looks like.

Use conversation to work through its steps. Develop repeatable help around the tasks and tools involved. Your industry knowledge gives the automation its direction.

Explore industry examples

Less interruption. More continuity.

Once you grant standing permission for cloud assistance, ordinary calls covered by that permission should not keep stopping the job to ask the same question again.

A new decision may still need you. An old permission should not.

Thoughtful help is our direction.

We are developing an assistant that pays attention to your priorities and prepares useful next steps. An increasingly familiar working relationship, with initiative serving your goals rather than competing for your attention.

See the vision

You stay in charge.

Blue Spark is AI, not a literal human hire. Important work still deserves your judgment and review. The role of your assistant is to lighten the workload while keeping the decisions that matter in your hands.
