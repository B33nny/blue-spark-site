The product

Eleven capabilities. One persistent brain loop.

Get ProExplore the memory

The eleven capabilities.

  1. 01

    Voice

    Local speech-to-text, local text-to-speech. Low latency, scaled to your hardware.

  2. 02

    Memory

    Remembers you across sessions, fiscal periods, and case files. Five distinct layers of recall.

  3. 03

    Brain

    The thinking loop. Routes to your local model or to the cloud model only when reasoning demands it.

  4. 04

    Hands

    A worker process for the actions you consent to: keyboard, browser, files. Asks before anything outside the grant.

  5. 05

    Bridge

    The registry of integrated tools and external API hooks you trust the product to touch.

  6. 06

    Controls

    Permissions and the audit ledger that records them. The definitive kill switch that stops outbound action instantly.

  7. 07

    Skills

    Persona and persona revisions. The specific work skills you build over time.

  8. 08

    Status

    Live system health, memory indexing state, license, and version. One panel, visible anytime.

  9. 09

    Update

    Auto-update. Signed installer. Quiet by default.

  10. 10

    Initiative

    It takes its own initiative to research or prepare work for its user.

  11. 11

    Sleep

    It consolidates daily data and finds ways to improve ways it serves its user.

Neuromorphic memory

The five layers.

Blue Spark utilizes a five-layer memory architecture stored exclusively on your local disk:

Working
The active context of the current task.
Episodic
Time-stamped records of past events and specific turns in a conversation.
Semantic
Factual data about your preferences and environment. Recurring facts strengthen over time; irrelevant details fade.
Procedural
Learned sequences of actions. When a command is given, the system recalls the exact multi-step execution pattern required.
Persona
A foundational, long-lived model of your behavior, ensuring the assistant adapts to your specific communication style.
