Other AIs are a model with features. Blue Spark is a system with a model inside.

Most AI is a frozen brain in a box: one model, a memory that wipes when the chat ends, and upgrades only when someone retrains it. Blue Spark is built differently. Its memory lives on your own computer and grows with use. Its skills are earned. And the AI model at the centre is the one part designed to be replaceable.

The big difference

A frozen brain in a box, or a system that grows with you.

Every other AI

  • Forgets when the chat ends
  • Only learns when someone retrains it
  • The model is the whole product

Blue Spark

  • Remembers, on your own computer
  • Gets better between conversations
  • The model is one part you can swap

Part 1 of 5 · Memory

A memory that works like a nervous system, not a notebook.

Other AI either forgets everything when the chat ends, or pastes old chat back in. Blue Spark keeps five layers of memory. Memories get stronger when you use them and fade when you don't. Overnight, the important moments settle into lasting knowledge, a bit like sleep does for people.

Everything lives in one place on your own computer, so there is one version of the truth.

Part 2 of 5 · Recall

Three ways of remembering, combined.

When you ask something, Blue Spark searches by meaning, by keyword and by exact match, then blends the results. Meaning finds the idea. Keywords and exact match catch the names, numbers and codes that a meaning search alone can miss.

If one of the three searches can't run, it tells you, instead of pretending it looked everywhere.

Part 3 of 5 · Skills

Skills are earned, not assumed.

Blue Spark notices patterns in how you work and suggests new routines. A suggestion only becomes a skill after it passes checks. If a skill starts failing, it loses the right to run on its own.

You can also hand-craft skills for your own customers. They go through the same checks.

Part 4 of 5 · Routing

The right amount of brainpower for each job.

Quick questions go to a fast, low-cost model. Hard problems go to a stronger one. Blue Spark decides based on its own record of what worked before, so you never have to pick a model.

If one AI provider has a bad day, the work moves to another and carries on.

Part 5 of 5 · The Brain

It steps back and thinks things over.

Throughout the day, Blue Spark reviews what happened. It writes down what it learned and where it got stuck, so the next attempt starts smarter.

Before the main model answers you, a team of specialist advisers looks at the question first, each from its own angle.

The rule that holds it together

Nothing is true until it's checked.

An AI saying it finished a job doesn't count. Blue Spark only treats work as done once it has been checked against the real result. When it isn't sure, it says so and stops, instead of faking a confident answer.

You are the last resort, not the first. It comes to you when your decision is really needed. We call the whole system around the model Mahanta OS.

Fewer accidents

Ten ways AI goes wrong, and what stops each one.

In most AI, one model does the work, judges the work and remembers the work. Blue Spark splits those jobs up, so a mistake has to get past several separate parts before it reaches you.

  1. 01

    Makes the same mistake next week

    It remembers what went wrong, and only keeps lessons that were checked.

    Stopped by memory
  2. 02

    Says "done" when it isn't

    A claim of success doesn't count until the result is checked.

    Stopped by the check rule
  3. 03

    Retries the same failing step

    It remembers what failed, so the next try has to be different.

    Stopped by memory
  4. 04

    Gets blocked and hands back half a job

    It works out why it was blocked and changes approach, or tells you plainly.

    Stopped by the check rule
  5. 05

    The AI provider goes down mid-job

    The work moves to another provider and carries on.

    Stopped by routing
  6. 06

    Data goes where it shouldn't

    Search and memory stay on your computer. If privacy is unclear, it refuses and says why.

    Stopped by staying local
  7. 07

    Misses the right memory

    Three ways of searching, and a warning if one of them couldn't run.

    Stopped by recall
  8. 08

    Learns the wrong lesson

    No check, no skill. A routine that fails is taken off duty.

    Stopped by skills
  9. 09

    Shows all green while something's broken

    It would rather show an old status than a false all-clear.

    Stopped by honest status
  10. 10

    One model has a bad day

    Your memory and skills don't depend on any one model.

    Stopped by the design
