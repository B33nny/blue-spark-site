What we've shipped. What we plan. What we won't.

We've shipped an early version. We have a plan for the next twelve months. We have a list of things we are deliberately not doing, with reasons. Each row has a date we are willing to defend out loud.

If a row on this page stops being true, the page gets updated.

Product and architecture, quarter by quarter.

R3 — What we won't do, and why

Decisions we are deliberately not making.

No medical field in v1.

The consent and liability surface is too wide for a v1 product to cover credibly. We revisit at roughly five thousand paid Pro users. Until then, this category stays out — stated as confidence, not weakness.

No manufactured urgency anywhere.

No fake "two minutes left" banners. No "X people viewing." No pre-checked opt-ins. No decoy tiers. No hidden auto-renewal.

No SaaS-bill framing for Pro.

Pro is the cost of keeping your spark bright, not a monthly bill. The framing holds on every page.

Promise 01

The kill switch is the last to fail.

The sovereignty overrides work. They don't degrade under load.

Promise 02

The local store has one copy.

Memory, conversations, audit ledger, persona — one place, one machine. No replication, no drift.

Promise 03

The license check is the only ungranted request.

Everything else happens locally, and the license check is opt-out-able in the offline grace period.
