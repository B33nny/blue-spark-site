Get Blue Spark

An assistant that grows with you.

More familiar. More thoughtful. More useful.

Better help, not simply more features.

Our direction is a continuing assistant that understands more of your work: the preferences you have explained, the projects you return to, and the experience of working alongside you.

We want familiarity to make assistance more useful — not just make the conversation longer.

Prepare, not just respond.

We are developing Blue Spark's initiative: recognising useful next steps and preparing for the priorities you have shared, within the authority you give it.

The purpose is less for you to carry. Not more noise to manage.

Let professional knowledge become shared capability.

A skills marketplace is planned for 2027, shaped with input from the Founding 500. It is intended to help people share, sell, or rent useful Blue Spark skills.

Clear about today. Thoughtful about tomorrow.

This page describes our direction, not a list of features already delivered. Product information describes current capabilities; our public vision does not disclose the proprietary build plan.
