Help when something is not working as expected.

Status unavailable

Status unavailable. A live status feed for Blue Spark services is not published at this time. Check this page for changes as we add connected services and reporting.

Account, licence, or connected-service issue?

Tell support what failed, the message you saw, and approximately when it happened. Keep passwords and private work out of the initial report.

Installation or local computer issue?

Your computer's condition is separate from external service availability. Include your operating-system and Blue Spark versions when asking for help.

Continuing an enquiry?

Use the same conversation and add any new error or change in behaviour. That keeps the relevant information together.
