Honest should mean something.

Not simply sound reassuring.

Work you can understand.

A useful result should be distinguishable from an unfinished attempt. Progress should not be presented as completion. Problems should not disappear behind confident language.

That is the standard we are building Blue Spark around.

Claims with a clear meaning.

A current capability and a future ambition are different. A demonstration should show the work it claims to show. Published results should explain their conditions rather than imply that every task will be identical.

Information without guesswork.

Local memory and cloud processing are not the same. We explain where selected task information can go and why the distinction matters.

Privacy and control

Terms you can read before choosing.

Monthly access should be described as monthly. Usage and licence conditions should be clear before payment. A future marketplace should not look like an operating shop.

Access information

Responsibility, not perfection.

AI can make mistakes. Honesty means taking that seriously: explaining the limits, listening to feedback, and improving what needs attention.
